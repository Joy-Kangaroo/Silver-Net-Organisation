Our Goals

Silver Net Organisation aims to connect isolated elderly people with other elderly citizens, allowing them to share their stories, thoughts, and gather inspiration from others. Many elderly people today struggle with communicating with others because of their mobility or detoriating sensory ability, keeping them in their homes. This prevents them from soialising with others, the key tool to keep their brains active. This is the main cause of of depression in elderly people, and accelerates the detoriation of their health. Silver Net Organisation strives to improve the lives and reduce the depression of elderly people by connecting them to other they can relate to, enabling them to socialise and keep their mind and sensors active. We hope to improve the daily lives and life-quality of the increasing amount of elderly people.
